# SpotLab product guide

## Core loop

SpotLab teaches poker decisions through short, repeatable scenarios:

1. Read the positions, stack, pot, board, and prior actions.
2. Choose one legal action and sizing.
3. Follow the analysis stages.
4. Compare the selected action with the highest-EV line and strategy mix.
5. Open the explanation or range lab when deeper context is useful.
6. Continue to the next scenario and build rating history.

## Battle

Battle is the home destination. It surfaces the daily challenge, quick sessions, rating summary, and the next useful practice mode. The daily challenge is a short featured hand with a non-cash rating reward.

## Missions

Missions is the scenario library. Players can switch between MTT and cash contexts and filter by street. Each mission previews the matchup, effective stack, pot, board, difficulty, reward, and completion state before opening the full table.

## Trainer

The trainer is built around a six-seat table. Faded seats preserve position context without competing with the two active players. The action history explains how the hand reached the current decision.

The decision deck translates each option into poker language:

- **Fold** exits the hand.
- **Check** continues without wagering.
- **Call** shows the amount required to continue.
- **Bet** shows both pot percentage and BB amount.
- **Raise** shows the final raise-to amount.

Submitting an action locks the alternatives while the practice engine evaluates ranges, expected value, mixed strategies, and coaching context.

## Review

The result view prioritizes the learning sequence:

1. decision score and rating change;
2. selected action versus best line;
3. full-range strategy frequencies and EV;
4. solver insight and expanded reasoning;
5. range exploration;
6. next scenario.

Close decisions are treated as strategic tradeoffs rather than simple binary failures.

## Range lab

The range lab presents all 169 starting-hand classes. Color represents the dominant action, bar length represents frequency, and selection updates the hand inspector. The matrix is a compact educational view of the included fixture data and does not claim solver verification.

## League

League turns completed decisions into a persistent skill profile. It includes rating, tier, average EV loss, weekly progress, skill categories, a priority training area, and recent reviews. Training links connect weaker areas back to relevant missions.

## Rating and rewards

Rating, XP, stars, and tier progress are training feedback only. SpotLab contains no cash balance, wagering wallet, purchasable chips, or real-money reward mechanic.
